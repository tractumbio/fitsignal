=== GITHUB / LATEX TEMPLATES ===
- Awesome-CV (posquit0) — clean, widely used LaTeX CV/résumé/cover-letter template: https://github.com/posquit0/Awesome-CV
- Deedy-Resume — one-page two-column XeTeX template (dense; prefer single-column variant for ATS safety): https://github.com/deedy/Deedy-Resume | Overleaf: https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/deedy-cv/bjryvfsjdyxz
- Enhanced-Deedy (reversed layout): https://github.com/harshad-kadam/Enhanced-Deedy_Resume
- Awesome-PhD-CV (LimHyungTae) — curated templates for converting academic CVs to ATS-safe industry resumes; includes Jake's Resume (single-page, ATS-optimized): https://github.com/LimHyungTae/Awesome-PhD-CV
- Browse more: https://github.com/topics/latex-resume-template | https://github.com/topics/resume-template
TEMPLATE CHOICE FOR MBB: single-column, minimalist, black-and-white. Two-column templates (Deedy) look modern but can break ATS parsers and deviate from consulting norms — prefer Jake's Resume / Awesome-CV single-column or a plain Word template from a firm/coaching source.
